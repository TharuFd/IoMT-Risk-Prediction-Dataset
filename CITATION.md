# How to cite

**Citation metadata must be verified before assigning authorship.** Suggested plain-text format (replace placeholders only with verified details):

> [Verified author(s)]. ([Publication year]). *IoMT Attack Detection and Next-24h Risk Prediction Dataset* (version [version]). [Repository URL]. [DOI if registered].

Associated research: *Hybrid AI-based Detection of Fileless Malware in IoMT Healthcare Systems* (MediRecon).

Do not cite the dataset as a validated real-world predictive benchmark unless the labeling protocol and external validation are documented.
